What the app does

A property report built for development decisions, not just browsing.

Ask about an address or search for opportunities. Project Alpha gathers structured property context, runs development heuristics, and presents the tradeoffs in language a buyer, investor, or professional team can act on.

01

Site feasibility

Surface zoning, land area, title type, overlays, terrain, services, and subdivision pathway notes in one mobile-first report.

02

Cost and ROI scenarios

Compare development cost ranges, holding costs, likely exit cases, and ROI sensitivity before spending money on deeper due diligence.

03

Professional referrals

When a user needs an architect, planner, engineer, quantity surveyor, or related expert, the app can help connect them with a relevant provider.

Workflow

From curiosity to a clearer next step.

Project Alpha is designed for the early decision window: when a property looks interesting, but you need to know what could make it viable, expensive, risky, or worth investigating.

1

Ask or search

Enter an address, ask a property question, or search for development sites by suburb and budget.

2

Analyse

The app checks planning, title context, service constraints, terrain, and comparable signals.

3

Compare tradeoffs

Read score summaries, risk notes, cost ranges, and ROI scenarios without spreadsheet wrangling.

4

Bring in help

When the opportunity is worth pursuing, connect with a relevant property professional.

Who it is for

For people evaluating residential development opportunities.

Project Alpha supports both sides of the property decision: users who need clarity, and professionals who can help them move from analysis to action.

Buyers, developers, and investors

Screen addresses, understand major constraints, and decide whether a property deserves deeper diligence before committing time and fees.

Agents and service providers

Present property potential more clearly, receive relevant referral opportunities, and meet users at the moment they need expert guidance.
